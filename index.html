Why PadelDesk

Built for the person who actually organises the group

🪑 Sit-outs that are actually fair

With 10 players on 2 courts, somebody always rests. PadelDesk spreads rests evenly, never makes the same player sit twice in a row, and shows the whole group who rests next — so nobody argues with the organiser.

⏱️ Finish on time

Tell the planner how long you have the courts, and it suggests the rounds and points that fit. A round timer keeps every court moving so the session doesn't get cut off mid-match.

💬 Made for WhatsApp groups

Paste your sign-up list straight from the group chat — numbers and emojis are cleaned up automatically. When you're done, copy a formatted results message back to the group in one tap.

🔒 Private by default

Everything runs in your browser. No account, nothing uploaded. Share results with first names only if you prefer — full names never have to leave your phone.

🏆 Weekly group? Keep a season table

Save each session's results on your device and build a running leaderboard across weeks. Perfect for a regular Tuesday-night crew.

🚶 Latecomers welcome

Someone arrives at round 3? Someone has to leave early? Add or pause players mid-session and the rotation stays fair for everyone else.
