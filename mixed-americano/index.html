How mixed Americano works

A mixed Americano — often called Mixicano — is the social format for mixed groups. Every round you get a new partner of the opposite gender and new opponents. You collect points individually, and the player with the most points at the end wins. It's the most popular format for couples' nights and mixed club socials.

To set genders, either tag names in your list as Sara (f) and Ben (m), or set them in the preview table after you paste. The generator then builds every pair as one man + one woman.

Best with equal numbers

Mixed Americano is cleanest with an equal count of men and women — 4+4, 6+6, 8+8. With an uneven split the generator keeps as many pairs mixed as possible and warns you when a round can't be fully mixed. For a totally even night, aim for the same number of each.

Other formats

Want partners to rotate without the gender rule? Use a standard Americano. Want pairings by skill so matches get closer each round? Try a Mexicano. Coming with a fixed partner? Use Team Americano.

Quick answers

Is Mixicano the same as mixed Americano?
Yes. "Mixicano" is just a shorter name for a mixed Americano — every pair is one man and one woman, with rotating partners and individual scoring.
How many players do I need?
At least 4 (two men, two women). It scales to 40. Equal numbers of men and women give the cleanest pairings.
Does it handle sit-outs?
Yes — with odd counts the generator rotates rests fairly, avoids back-to-back benching, and can credit resters the round average.
