How many rounds (and points) for your Americano?

The question every organiser asks at 5pm: "We have the courts for 90 minutes — how many rounds do we play, and to how many points?" Here's the maths, ready to use.

How long one round takes

A padel point lasts roughly 30–45 seconds with changeovers and serve swaps. In practice, these per-round timings (including the switch between rounds) hold up well at club level:

Padel rounds run long more often than short — an Americano that ends ten minutes early is a great night; one cut off mid-round with no winner is the thing players remember. When in doubt, plan one round fewer and let the timer keep courts honest.

The "perfect" round counts by group size

  • 8 players, 2 courts: 7 rounds — everyone partners everyone exactly once. The mathematically perfect Americano.
  • 10 players, 2 courts: any multiple of 5 rounds — in 5 rounds each player rests exactly once.
  • 12 players, 3 courts: 11 rounds for the full partner cycle; 6–8 rounds is the realistic sweet spot.
  • 16 players, 4 courts: full cycle is 15 rounds (never happens) — 6–8 rounds gives great variety.
