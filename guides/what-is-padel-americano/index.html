What is Padel Americano?

The Americano is padel's favourite social format: partners rotate every round, you score points individually, and by the end everyone has played with and against almost everyone. One winner, zero pre-made teams, maximum mixing.

The rules in 60 seconds

  • Pairs rotate. Every round you get a new partner and new opponents, following a schedule set before the first ball.
  • Matches are played to a points total — commonly 16, 21, 24 or 32 — not to sets. Serve usually changes every 4 points; at the end a match might finish 12–9 or 17–4.
  • You keep your pair's score as personal points. Lose 12–9 and you still bank 9. Every point matters, which is exactly why Americanos stay competitive and friendly at the same time.
  • Most points after the last round wins. Ties are usually broken by wins, then point difference.

What you need

At least 4 players and one court per 4. The magic numbers are multiples of four — 8, 12, 16 — because nobody has to sit out. With other counts (10 players on 2 courts, say) players take turns resting; a good generator spreads those rests fairly and never benches the same player twice in a row.

Why clubs and WhatsApp groups love it

  • No partner needed — you sign up alone, the format does the rest. That's why clubs run weekly Americanos as their main social night.
  • Mixed levels work. Rotation means a weaker player isn't stuck carrying or being carried all night. (If levels vary a lot, consider a Mexicano instead.)
  • It fits a booking. Points-based matches have predictable length, so a 2-hour slot = a known number of rounds. The maths is in How many rounds?

Popular variations

Mexicano — pairings follow the live ranking from round 2, so matches get closer every round. Mixed Americano (Mixicano) — every pair is one man + one woman. Team Americano — fixed pairs all night, round robin between teams. All four run free in our Americano generator.

How to run one tonight (checklist)

  1. Post a sign-up list in the group chat and book one court per 4 players.
  2. Paste the list into the generator — numbering and emojis are cleaned automatically.
  3. Pick points per match with the session planner so you finish on time.
  4. Read each round out (or share the link), type scores as courts finish.
  5. Copy the final standings to WhatsApp with one tap. Done.
