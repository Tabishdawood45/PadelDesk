Padel Level Converter

Booked a court abroad, or joining a match in a new app? See what your Playtomic level roughly means as an LTA padel rating (UK) or USPA rating (US) — plus honest guidance for the French FFT system.

How each system actually works

Playtomic level (0–7)

You answer a questionnaire when you join, then the algorithm moves your level up or down after every competitive match based on results and opponents. It's the most widely used number in European amateur padel because Playtomic also handles court booking and open matches.

LTA padel rating (Great Britain, 1.0–6.5+)

The LTA's system is a standardised self-assessment: you pick the statement that best describes your game ("I lack technique but have some control…" and so on) and it maps to a rating used for club divisions and match arranging. Competent tennis or squash players who are new to padel typically enter around 3.5.

USPA rating (United States, 1.0–7.0)

The United States Padel Association uses an NTRP-style 1.0–7.0 scale for sanctioned events and league brackets — familiar territory if you've played rated tennis in the US.

France: FFT classement — a ranking, not a level

France is different. The FFT publishes a monthly national ranking (classement) computed from tournament results. The P25/P100/P250/P500/P1000/P2000 labels you see everywhere are tournament categories, not player levels — "P100" describes the event, not the player. So there is no official "FFT level" to convert to; the right-hand column above shows the kind of FFT event where each ability band usually competes comfortably. If you play FFT tournaments, your actual classement is on your FFT licence profile.

Why this matters

Mis-stated levels are the #1 complaint in open matches: a "3.5" from one app can be a "2.5" in another, and the match is ruined for four people. When you travel — a padel trip to Spain, a work move to London or Miami — knowing your rough equivalent keeps matches fun and fair. Organising for a group of mixed levels? Frequently asked questions

Is this conversion official?
No — none of these organisations publish official equivalences, and Playtomic states it cannot import levels from other systems. This chart aligns the published descriptions of each scale by playing ability. Use it as a starting point and adjust after a match or two.
Why is my Playtomic level different from how I'd rate myself?
Playtomic only moves on competitive results inside Playtomic. If you mostly play friendly matches, or play outside the app, your number can lag behind your real level in either direction.
What level do I need for my first tournament?
In the UK, club tournaments usually state an LTA rating band. In France, start with a P25 or P100 — they're designed for newer competitors. In the US, USPA events are bracketed by rating. Around Playtomic 2.5–3.0 you're ready to enjoy entry-level competition anywhere.
