How the padel Americano generator works

  1. Paste your player list. Copy the sign-up list straight from your WhatsApp group — "1. Ali ✅", bullets and emojis are cleaned up automatically. 4 to 40 players.
  2. Pick format, courts and points. Americano, Mexicano, Mixed Americano or Team Americano, on 1–10 courts, playing to 16, 21, 24 or 32 points per match. Not sure what fits your booking? The session planner turns "90 minutes, 2 courts, 10 players" into a ready-made plan.
  3. Play. Each round shows who plays with whom on which court, plus who's resting and who rests next. Type scores at the court — the second score fills in automatically and the leaderboard updates live.
  4. Share. One tap copies a formatted results message for the group chat, or a read-only link anyone can open. Running a weekly group? Save each session into a season table.

Why organisers use PadelDesk instead of a spreadsheet

  • Fair sit-outs, visibly fair. With 10 players on 2 courts, two people rest every round. The generator spreads rests evenly, never benches anyone twice in a row, and shows the whole group who rests next — so the organiser never gets the blame.
  • Odd numbers are fine. 5, 7, 9, 11 players — no problem. Resting players can be credited the round average so one rest doesn't ruin their ranking.
  • Latecomers and early leavers. Add a player at round 3 or pause someone who has to go — the rotation recalculates and stays fair for everyone else.
  • Skill balancing (optional). Give players a rough level from 1–10 and the generator keeps matches close instead of 8–0 blowouts.
  • Finish on time. The round timer (with auto-restart and a beep) keeps all courts moving, so your Americano doesn't get cut off mid-round when the booking ends.
  • Private by default. No account, nothing uploaded. Share with first names only if your group prefers.

Americano, Mexicano, Mixed or Team — which one tonight?

Americano is the classic social format: partners rotate every round, you score points individually, and everyone plays with everyone. Best for groups of mixed ability who want maximum variety.

Mexicano starts with a random round, then pairs players by the live ranking — 1st and 3rd play 2nd and 4th on court 1, and so on. Matches get closer every round, which makes it great when skill levels vary a lot. Read the full comparison in our Americano vs Mexicano guide.

Mixed Americano (sometimes called Mixicano) keeps every pair one man + one woman. Tag names with (m)/(f) in your list, or set genders in the preview table.

Team Americano keeps fixed pairs all night and runs a round robin between teams — ideal when people bring their regular partner.

Frequently asked questions

What is a padel Americano?
A social padel tournament where partners rotate every round and points are collected individually. Matches are usually played to a fixed total (16, 21, 24 or 32 points); the pair's score goes to each player. After the last round, the individual with the most points wins. More detail in What is Padel Americano?
What's the difference between Americano and Mexicano?
Americano fixes the whole schedule up front so everyone plays with everyone. Mexicano re-pairs every round based on the live ranking, so matches get tighter as the night goes on. See the full comparison.
Can I run an Americano with 5, 9 or any odd number of players?
Yes — that's exactly what this tool is built for. The players who rest are rotated fairly: everyone rests the same number of times (within one), nobody rests twice in a row, and you can credit resters the round average so the standings stay fair.
How many rounds and how many points should we play?
It depends on your booking. As a rule of thumb a 16-point match takes ~12 minutes, 21 points ~15, 24 points ~17 and 32 points ~22. Enter your minutes in the session planner above and it lays out the options. Full maths in How many rounds?
Do players need the app or an account?
No app, no account, ever. It's a web page that works on any phone. Scores live in your browser only; sharing is always your choice.
Can other people see the live scores?
You can copy a read-only snapshot link at any moment and drop it in the group chat. Turn on "first names only" and full names never leave your device.
Does it work offline at the club?
Once the page is open it keeps working without a connection — scores save to your device. Load the page before you head into a basement court, and avoid closing the tab.
