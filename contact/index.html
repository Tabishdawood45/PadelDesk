Contact

Feedback makes the tools better — especially from people who actually run group nights.

Email: contact@padeldesk.org

We read everything. Useful things to include:

  • Bug reports: your phone/browser, the tool, and what happened.
  • Feature ideas: how your group plays and what's missing.
  • Clubs & coaches: tell us how you run your socials — club-focused features are on the roadmap.
  • Partnerships / advertising: brief details of your product and audience.

PadelDesk stores no user accounts and no player data server-side, so support questions about "my data" usually have a happy answer: it's on your device, under your control.
