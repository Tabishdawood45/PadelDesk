Keeping 4 courts in sync

The organiser's real job at this size is logistics: four matches finishing at different times. Two tricks keep it tight: play to a fixed points total (all courts finish within a couple of minutes) and run the round timer so a marathon deuce can't hold up 12 people. Both are built into the generator.

Variety maths

15 rounds would let everyone partner everyone — far more than one night. In 6–8 rounds each player still gets a new partner every round, and our optimiser keeps repeated opponents rare.

Timing

6 rounds of 21 ≈ 100 min; 8 rounds of 16 ≈ 110 min. For a relaxed 2.5-hour event: 7 rounds of 21 with a drinks break.

Quick answers

How many rounds for 16 padel players?
Six to eight rounds on 4 courts. All 16 play every round; the complete 15-round partner cycle is for leagues, not evenings.
What if we have 16 players but only 3 courts?
Four rest each round — everyone rests every fourth round. The generator rotates rests fairly and shows who sits next. Or split into two groups of 8 for two perfect 7-round Americanos.
Americano or Mexicano for 16?
Sixteen mixed-level players is Mexicano territory: after two rounds the courts naturally sort by standard — court 1 becomes the "top table" and every court gets close matches.
