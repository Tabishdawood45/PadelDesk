Padel Americano for 8 players

Why 8 is the magic number

Each player has 7 possible partners. With all 8 on court every round (2 courts × 4 players), a well-built schedule rotates partners so that after 7 rounds every pair has happened exactly once — 14 matches, zero repeats, zero sit-outs. Our generator finds that perfect rotation automatically.

Timing

7 rounds of 16 points ≈ 100 minutes — fits a 2-hour booking with warm-up. Only have 90 minutes? Play 6 rounds of 16, or 5 of 21. The in-tool planner does this for you.

Quick answers

How many rounds for 8 padel players?
Seven rounds is the full cycle — every player partners every other player exactly once, with no repeats and no sit-outs. With less time, 5–6 rounds still give excellent variety.
How many courts do 8 players need?
Two. All 8 play every round, 4 per court.
Can we play 8 players on one court?
Yes, but 4 rest each round, so you double the session length for the same padel. If only one court is available, consider splitting into two time slots or playing a 4-player Americano twice.
