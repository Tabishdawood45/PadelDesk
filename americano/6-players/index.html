Padel Americano for 6 players

Six players on one court is a classic small Americano — 4 play, 2 rest each round. With a fair rotation everyone gets near-equal court time and plays with everyone.

How 6 players works on one court

With 6 players and one court, two people sit out every round. Over 3 rounds everyone has rested once (2 × 3 = 6). So multiples of 3 rounds keep rests perfectly even. Our generator spreads the sit-outs, never benches the same person twice in a row, and shows who rests next — and can give resting players the round average so one rest doesn't sink their ranking.

Got a second court?

With 2 courts all 6 can't play at once anyway (that needs 8), so one court is right for 6. If a 7th or 8th person turns up, add them mid-session and the generator rebalances — or jump to the 8-player setup, which needs no sit-outs at all.

Timing

7 rounds of 21 points ≈ 100 minutes, a good fit for a 2-hour booking with warm-up. Short on time? 5–6 rounds of 16 points.

Quick answers

How many rounds for 6 padel players?
Plan 7–9 rounds. Every 3 rounds each player has rested once, so multiples of 3 keep rests even. 7–9 rounds give lots of partner variety without dragging on.
Can 6 players play without anyone resting?
No — one court seats 4, so 2 always rest. The rotation shares those rests equally. If you want zero sit-outs, you need 8 players on 2 courts.
Is 6 players better as a Mexicano?
If levels vary, yes. A Mexicano re-pairs by ranking each round so matches get closer. Rests rotate the same way. Try the Mexicano format in the generator.
