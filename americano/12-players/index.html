How the rotation works

Each player has 11 possible partners, so the complete everyone-partners-everyone cycle is 11 rounds — more than an evening allows. In practice 6–8 rounds give every player a fresh partner each round with very few repeated opponents; our generator optimises each round so repeats stay minimal.

Only have 2 courts?

Then 8 play and 4 rest each round — each player rests every third round. It works, but it halves momentum; with 12 players, three courts is worth fighting for.

Timing

6 rounds of 21 ≈ 100 minutes. A 2-hour slot comfortably fits 6–7 rounds of 21 or 8 of 16.

Quick answers

How many rounds for 12 padel players?
Plan 6–8 rounds on 3 courts. The complete partner cycle would be 11 rounds, which rarely fits an evening; 6–8 already gives a different partner every round.
Can 12 players run on 2 courts?
Yes — 4 rest per round, i.e. everyone rests every third round. Fair, but slower. Three courts keeps all 12 playing.
Team Americano with 12 players?
Six fixed pairs, round robin: each team plays 5 matches. On 3 courts that’s exactly 5 rounds — a neat, self-contained night.
