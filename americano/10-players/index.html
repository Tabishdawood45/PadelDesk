Padel Americano for 10 players

Ten players on two courts is the most common "odd" Americano — 8 play, 2 rest each round. Run it well and nobody notices the resting; run it badly and it's all anyone talks about.

The sit-out maths

With 2 resting per round, after 5 rounds every player has rested exactly once (2 × 5 = 10). That makes 5 rounds — or 10, if you have the legs — the fair round counts. Our generator keeps rests even, never benches anyone twice in a row, shows who rests next, and can credit resters the round average so one rest doesn't wreck a ranking.

Timing

5 rounds of 21 points ≈ 85–90 minutes: the classic 90-minute, 2-court, 10-player club night. For a 2-hour slot, play 5 rounds of 24 or 7–8 of 16.

Quick answers

How does resting work with 10 players on 2 courts?
Two players rest each round. In 5 rounds everyone rests exactly once, so 5 (or 10) rounds keeps things perfectly fair. A good generator also makes sure nobody rests twice in a row.
Do resting players lose out on points?
They can — which is why PadelDesk offers rest compensation: resting players receive the round’s average points so the final standings stay fair.
Would 10 players be better as a Mexicano?
If skill levels vary a lot, yes — Mexicano pairs by live ranking so matches get closer every round. The rest rotation works the same way.
