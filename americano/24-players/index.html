This is an event, so plan it

With 24 players and 6 courts, treat it like a tournament: fixed points per match so courts finish together, a round timer on every court, and the results shared to a group so nobody loses track. The generator handles the draw and the live leaderboard; you handle the drinks.

Fewer courts?

On 5 courts, 20 play and 4 rest each round; on 4 courts, 16 play and 8 rest. Both work with fair rotation, but 24 players really wants six courts. Alternatively split into two 12-player Americanos on three courts each.

Timing

6 rounds of 21 ≈ 100 min. A relaxed 3-hour event fits 7–8 rounds with a break.

Quick answers

How many rounds for 24 padel players?
Six to eight rounds on 6 courts. All 24 play each round; the complete 23-round partner rotation is league territory.
How many courts for 24 players?
Six is ideal — everyone plays, nobody rests. With fewer courts some players rest each round; the generator shares those rests fairly.
Team Americano with 24 players?
Twelve fixed pairs on 6 courts run a clean round robin. Each team plays several matches and the leaderboard ranks the pairs.
