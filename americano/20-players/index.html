Running five courts smoothly

At this size the organiser's job is logistics, not maths. Two things keep it tight: play to a fixed points total so all courts finish together, and use the round timer so one slow court can't hold up the other sixteen players. Both are built into the generator.

Variety

A full everyone-partners-everyone rotation is 19 rounds — far more than an evening. In 6–8 rounds every player still gets a new partner each round and the optimiser keeps repeated opponents rare.

Timing

6 rounds of 21 ≈ 100 min; 8 rounds of 16 ≈ 110 min. For a 2.5-hour event, 7 rounds of 21 with a break.

Quick answers

How many rounds for 20 padel players?
Six to eight on 5 courts. All 20 play every round; the full 19-round rotation is for leagues, not one night.
What if we only have 4 courts for 20 players?
16 play and 4 rest each round — everyone rests every fifth round. The generator rotates the rests fairly and shows who sits next.
Americano or Mexicano for 20 players?
With mixed levels, Mexicano sorts the courts by standard after two rounds, so every court gets close matches and court 1 becomes the top table.
