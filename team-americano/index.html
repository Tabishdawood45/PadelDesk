Team Americano generator

Keep your fixed partner all night and run a fair round robin between teams — free, no app, no sign-up. Ideal when everyone brings a regular partner and wants to play together.

How team Americano works

In a team Americano, pairs stay fixed for the whole session. Each pair plays the other pairs in a round robin, points are added up per team, and the pair with the most points wins. It's the right format when people arrive as partners — couples, regular doubles, friends — and don't want to be split up.

Teammates are taken in list order: players 1 & 2 are a team, 3 & 4 are a team, and so on. Enter an even number of players and pick Team Americano in the format menu.

Team vs individual formats

Prefer rotating partners so everyone plays with everyone? Use a standard Americano. Want pairings by skill that tighten each round? Use a Mexicano. Want mixed man-and-woman pairs? Use a mixed Americano.

Quick answers

How many teams do I need?
At least 2 (4 players). With an even number of players the generator builds the pairs and runs a round robin so every team meets the others.
What if we have an odd number of players?
Team format needs even numbers so everyone has a partner. With an odd number, switch to a standard or mixed Americano, which handles sit-outs.
Does it keep a leaderboard?
Yes — scores are totalled per team and the live leaderboard ranks the pairs as you enter results.
