Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to padeldesk.org

The short version

PadelDesk's tools run in your browser. We have no user accounts and no server-side database of players or scores. Names and results you type are stored in your device's local storage only, and are deleted when you clear it. Sharing (links, WhatsApp text) happens only when you choose to share.

1. Data you enter into tools

Player names, scores, levels, season tables and settings are processed locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. They are not transmitted to PadelDesk. If you create a share link, the tournament snapshot is encoded inside the link itself — anyone you give the link to can read it, so use the "first names only" option if you prefer.

2. Cookies and advertising

We may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads and measure performance; in the EEA/UK, personalised advertising is only used with your consent, collected via a Google-certified consent banner. Google's use of advertising cookies is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and you can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

3. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, cookie-free aggregate analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to see which pages are used. This does not identify you.

4. Affiliate links

Some outbound links to equipment retailers are affiliate links: if you buy after clicking, the retailer may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate networks may set a cookie to attribute the sale. We only link to products in categories we genuinely cover.

5. Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply. We don't add you to lists or share your address.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR/UK GDPR you have rights of access, rectification and erasure. Because tool data lives on your own device, you can exercise most of these yourself by clearing the site's data in your browser. For anything else, contact contact@padeldesk.org.

7. Children

PadelDesk is a general-audience utility site and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change; the date above reflects the latest revision.
