About PadelDesk

PadelDesk is an independent project with one job: make the practical side of padel — organising, scoring, planning — free and frictionless for the millions of people discovering the sport.

What we believe

  • No app, no account, ever. The person organising Tuesday night padel shouldn't have to make 9 friends download anything. Every PadelDesk tool runs in the browser on any phone.
  • Private by default. Names and scores stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you deliberately share a link — and even then you can share first names only.
  • Fairness you can see. Our Americano engine spreads sit-outs evenly, never benches anyone twice in a row, and shows the whole group who rests next. The organiser should never have to defend the draw.
  • Accurate over hyped. Our guides reflect the current FIP rulebook (including the 2026 Star Point), and our calculators cite their sources and show their assumptions.

The tools

The Americano & Mexicano generator (fair rotations, live leaderboard, WhatsApp-ready results, season tables) · the score counter with all three official deuce modes · the level converter for Playtomic, LTA, USPA and FFT guidance · the court cost calculator for the UK, US and France · and the racket finder quiz.

Who makes PadelDesk

PadelDesk is an independent project built and maintained by Tabish, a web developer. It started after watching how much time friends lost organising their weekly padel sessions on paper and in group chats — the scheduling, the sit-outs, the arguments about who sat out last time. The tools here are the fix for exactly those problems, and they keep improving based on what real organisers ask for.

It isn't run by a club or a brand, and it's not affiliated with any federation or app. That independence is the point: the tools stay free, honest about what they can and can't do, and built for the person doing the organising rather than for a sponsor.

How the site is funded

PadelDesk is free for everyone and always will be. The site may show ads and may earn a commission when you buy gear through clearly marked outbound links — at no cost to you. That's the whole model: useful tools, honest funding.

Questions, ideas or a bug to report? Get in touch — organiser feedback shapes what gets built next.
